Project Title: Semantic Plagiarism Detection Agent
Domain: Education
Core Technologies: NLP, Embeddings, Semantic Search

1. Project Overview
The objective is to develop a semantic plagiarism detection system tailored for the education domain. Unlike traditional systems that rely heavily on exact word and phrase matching, this agent identifies content similarities based on underlying meaning. 2. Minimug core components:
Document/Text Processing: Modules to ingest, clean, and normalize submitted text documents.
Semantic Embeddings: Utilizing NLP models to convert textual data into high-dimensional vector representations that capture context.
Similarity Calculation: Mathematical functions to compute the distance between text embeddings and determine the degree of meaning overlap.
Paraphrase Detection: Algorithms specifically designed to recognize restructured sentences that retain the original semantic core.
Matching Section Identification: The ability to pinpoint and highlight specific regions within the text that exhibit high semantic similarity to source materials.
Similarity Percentage: An aggregate scoring mechanism that calculates the overall percentage of unoriginal content.
Plagiarism Report: Generation of a comprehensive, easily readable output document for educators detailing the findings.
